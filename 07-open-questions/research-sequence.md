# Research sequence

This is the intended sequence for the first-principles interview/research programme. It is a working plan, not a commitment to conclusions.

1. **Legitimate state power** — What powers should a government possess at all, regardless of whether it can exercise them efficiently?
2. **Political fairness** — What would it mean for a political system to treat citizens fairly?
3. **Electoral systems** — How should political preferences be converted into political power?
4. **Institutional constraints and small government** — How can legitimate limits on state power be made durable in practice?

Small-government ideas should be treated as a thread running through all four topics rather than assumed as a conclusion at the outset.

Discussion method:
- Begin from first principles.
- Use a conversational/interview format rather than drafting conclusions prematurely.
- Challenge assumptions and consider counterexamples.
- Keep factual claims distinct from normative judgements.
- Preserve the full discussion as a raw interview transcript.
- Extract conclusions, evidence questions, and research tasks separately after the discussion.
